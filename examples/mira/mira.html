Three things every generator gets wrong.

Mira is shaped around the moments where the workflow usually breaks — and turns them into something you can actually edit.

Edits stay alive

Every step is a non-destructive node. Re-run the prompt from yesterday, swap one parameter, keep the rest.

Real local control

Mask any region and re-prompt only that area. Texture, lighting, palette — without smearing the rest of the frame.

Side-by-side variants

Generate four at once, lock the two that work, branch new variants only from those. Less scroll-back, more progress.
